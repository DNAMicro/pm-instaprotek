# InstaProtek Support FAQ — Retail tab
Source: https://instaprotek.com/support (Retail tab), captured 2026-07-17.

## Registration

**How do I register my device?**
Two options: (1) Scan the instaProtek Shield's QR code on the product packaging — it directs to the appropriate app store to download the instaProtek app. (2) Download the app manually from the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android), search "instaProtek".

**Step-by-step registration:** done in the app (video guides for Android and iOS on the support page).

**What is the purchase receipt being asked to upload? Is it for my device?**
The receipt is for the screen protection purchased, NOT the device. It validates the purchase date and activates the protection plan.

**Trouble with the app during registration:** email support@instaprotek.com.

**Data deletion requests:** email support@instaprotek.com.

**Registration window:** register within 30 days from the product's purchase date.

## Warranty & Replacement

**How long does the limited warranty last?** 1 year from the date of purchase.

**Is the screen protector's replacement covered if damaged/faulty?**
- If the product comes with the instaProtek PRODUCT REPLACEMENT WARRANTY: yes, replacement of the screen protector is covered (shipping and handling fee applies).
- If the product comes with the instaProtek PRODUCT GUARANTEE (covers breakage of the device's screen): replacement of the screen protector is NOT covered.

**Claim eligibility:** product registered within 30 days of purchase AND device still within the 1-year limited warranty.

## Claim Process

**How to file a claim:** log in to the app > "File a claim" button, or email support@instaprotek.com.

**Important:** file the claim and submit photo(s) of the damaged device through the app BEFORE repairing the device. Photo of the broken device must display the IMEI/serial number.

**After repair approval:** max 30 days to get the device repaired and submit the repair receipt, or the one-time claim closes.

**Repair receipt must include:** repair store name and phone number; repair date; IMEI (smartphones) or serial number (tablets/wearables); device make and model; type of repair performed; amount paid.

**Submit the receipt by:** uploading a photo in the instaProtek app, or emailing it to support@instaprotek.com. InstaProtek validates the receipt with the repair store; reimbursement status comes via email or in the app.

**Reimbursement methods:** ACH transfer; PayPal (PayPal may charge their own processing fees); Check (a $12.99 processing fee applies). Select the preferred method within 30 days of approval or the one-time claim may close.

**Coverage amount:** varies by product purchased — shown in the registered account after registration or on the product packaging.
