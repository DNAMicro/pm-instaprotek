# Support canned responses (InstaProtek)

Approved reply templates. Match the inquiry to a rule and use that response closely.
Personalize the greeting with the customer's first name if known (otherwise "Hello,").
Keep the wording; never add refunds, replacements, or other concessions.

## 1. Device make/model & product-list confusion
Triggers (any): asks about entering device make and model; the "product list"; "cannot find
company"; "cannot find brand"; "cannot find product"; "add device"; "add model"; "add brand".

Hello,

Thanks for reaching out. The list is for your device where you installed the app - it is not
referring to the product. The product is already recognized when you scan the barcode. Please
try entering your phone's make and model and you should be able to proceed.

Best,
InstaProtek Support

## 2. Default (catch-all) - use for ANYTHING not clearly matching #1 or #3
Triggers: empty body; photo/attachment only; unclear or vague request; or any inquiry not
covered by an approved answer. When in doubt, use this - do NOT compose a bespoke reply.

Hello,

Thanks for reaching out. How can we help you? Please share a few details about the issue you're
seeing (a screenshot helps too) and we'll take it from there.

Best,
InstaProtek Support

## 3. Connection / server errors
Triggers (any): "cannot connect to server"; "unable to connect"; "connection error"; anything
about the app not connecting.

Hello,

Thanks for reaching out. Is this still an issue? If so, please uninstall and reinstall the app to
refresh its connection to the server, then register again.

Best,
InstaProtek Support
